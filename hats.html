Set Design or Custom

The hat, the leather, and the press are the same either way. What changes is who decides what the patch says.

Set design. The wording is already cut. You pick the hat and the color, and that is the build — no name swaps, no wording changes, no changes to the patch. It is cheaper because there is no proof to draw and nothing to approve.

Custom. Your department crest, your union local, or your own artwork. We email a proof before anything gets cut, and there are twenty-six hat colors to pick from instead of two. Crew discounts start at five hats.

Want something on a set design changed? Buy the Custom Leather Patch Hat instead. That is what it is there for.

What You Get

  • Genuine leather patch, laser-cut and engraved, then heat-pressed on
  • Richardson 112 trucker or performance waterproof hat
  • Adjustable snapback, one size
  • Built to order, 1–3 week lead time
