CREWS & DEPARTMENTS

Gear for the Whole House.

Matching pieces for a shift, a company, or a department. Same leather, same hand, same stamps down the line.

Most of what leaves this shop is one piece for one firefighter. But a good bit of it goes out in batches: eight helmet bands for a shift, a strap for every new lieutenant, thirty hats for a department. Crew work is the same leather and the same hands, just repeated until the whole house matches.

Tell us what the occasion is and we'll build to it.

What Crews Order

Promotions

New rank, new strap. Name and rank hand-stamped and painted, station number down the leather. Order one at a time as they come, or a set when the list posts.

Retirements

Years of service, badge number, the house they finished in. Stamped into full-grain leather that outlasts a plaque on a wall.

Memorials

Names, dates, and the marks that matter. We take this work seriously and we'll walk through every detail with you before anything is cut.

Station Orders

Helmet bands, glove straps, chin straps, or hats for everyone on the roster. Department crest or union local on every piece, each one named to the firefighter carrying it.

Crew Pricing

Patch hats have crew pricing built in. Add them to the cart and the discount comes off on its own — no code, no back and forth.

  • 10 or more hats: 10% off
  • 20 or more hats: 15% off
  • 30 or more hats: 20% off

Mix any colors and styles. The discount counts every hat in your cart. While the sitewide 20% off sale is running you get whichever is better, so right now every hat is 20% off regardless of count.

On everything else — radio straps, belts, helmet bands, glove straps, chin straps — send us the count and we'll come back with a price and a timeline. Batch work on those is quoted per order, because what a crew needs varies: eight identical helmet bands is a different job than eight straps with eight different names and three different stamps.

How It Works

  1. Tell us what you need. How many, what pieces, what goes on them, and whether there's a date attached.
  2. We come back with a price and a timeline. If the date won't work, we'll say so up front instead of after you've committed.
  3. You approve the design. Nothing gets cut until you've signed off on the stamps, the colors, and the spelling of every name.
  4. We build the batch and ship it together. One box, one tracking number, sorted so you're not matching names to pieces on the apparatus floor.

Lead Time

Everything is made to order, one at a time, by hand. Standard lead times are 6 weeks for radio straps and 1–3 weeks for belts, helmet bands, glove straps, chin straps, and hats.

A batch takes longer than a single piece, and how much longer depends on the size of the order and what's already on the bench. If you're working toward a promotion ceremony, a retirement dinner, or a memorial service, give us the date when you first reach out. We'll tell you straight whether it's doable.
