Our Leather

Leather is a natural material, and no two hides are the same. Grain, color, small scars, and the depth of a stamp will vary from piece to piece and from the photos on this site. The photos show work we've done; yours is built to match, not copied exactly. Colors on a screen won't match leather in hand. None of this is a defect. It's the material.

Dyed leather can rub color off onto clothing or skin, most of all when it's new or wet. Leather also needs care to last: condition it, and keep painted lettering from being bent over and over. Our Care & Upkeep section covers it.
