POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Rawhide City Leather ("we," "us," "our") is a small, firefighter-owned leather shop in Lakeland, Florida. This page explains what we collect when you visit rawhidecityleather.com, place an order, or get in touch, what we do with it, and who else handles it. The short version: we collect what we need to build and ship your gear, we don't sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it.

Information We Collect

Order information: your name, billing and shipping address, email, phone number, what you ordered, and the options you picked. Payment is handled by our checkout provider, Snipcart, and its payment processor, Stripe, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. We never see or store your full card number.

Your account: if you make an account, your name, email, date of birth, shipping address, and phone number if you give one. We use your date of birth to send your birthday code and to confirm you're 18 or older. If you save a card, Stripe keeps it; we only see the card brand, the last four digits, and the expiry date.

Custom order details: whatever you give us to build a custom piece, such as names, station numbers, badge designs, colors, and measurements.

Artwork and photos: if you upload a logo, photo, or sketch with an order or through the contact form, we store the file where only the shop can open it, and keep it with the order or message it came with.

Department orders: for a crew or department quote, the department's name and contact person, and for a tax-exempt order, the exemption certificate details.

Messages: anything you send us through the contact form or by email.

Site usage: details your browser sends automatically when a page loads, like your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, and the page that sent you here.

How We Use It

We use your information to take your payment, build and ship your order, and keep you posted on it. We use it to answer your questions and handle warranty claims, to keep the records the law requires (sales tax, for one), to keep the site secure and running, and to see which pages and ads are working.

If you join our email list, we send you new builds, restocks, and shop sales. If you start a checkout and leave before finishing, we may email you a reminder or two, sometimes with a discount code. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link, and one message to us stops them.

If you make an account, we email you a code to confirm your email address, a code whenever you reset your password, your sign-up discount code, and a birthday code once a year. You can turn the birthday email off on your account page. Your account page shows your orders, their tracking, and your codes, and fills in your email and address at checkout.

Who Else Handles It

We use a handful of outside services to run the shop. Each one gets only what it needs to do its job:

  • Snipcart runs our cart and checkout and holds your order.
  • Stripe processes your payment, including Apple Pay and Google Pay, and keeps any card you save to your account.
  • Pirate Ship prints our shipping labels and emails you tracking. UPS and USPS deliver the package. They get your name, shipping address, and email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and stores uploaded artwork, photos, and your account.
  • Brevo delivers our order, shipping, reminder, and account emails and the messages sent through our contact form. Kit runs our newsletter list.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) measures our ads, and Google measures site traffic with Google Analytics, serves our fonts, and runs Google Customer Reviews. More on both below.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to prevent fraud, or if the shop is ever sold, in which case this policy goes with it.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Ads and Measurement

Meta Pixel. Our pages run Meta's pixel, which sets cookies and tells Meta when you visit, view a product, add to cart, start a checkout, or buy. When you place an order, our server also sends Meta a record of the purchase (the order number, total, and products) with your email, name, phone number, and billing city, state, ZIP, and country in hashed (scrambled) form, plus your browser details and Meta's cookie IDs. Meta uses this to tell us which ads led to a sale and to show our ads to people more likely to want them.

Google Customer Reviews. After checkout, Google may show a pop-up offering a short survey about your order. To show it, we pass Google your email address, order number, country, and an estimated delivery date. If you opt in, Google emails you the survey after your order should have arrived and uses your answers for our seller rating on Google. If you don't, no survey is sent. Google handles this under its own privacy policy.

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to see how people find and use the site: which pages get visited, where visitors come from, and which visits end in an order. It sets cookies and tells Google which pages you view and what you add to your cart or buy, along with your browser, device, and rough location. Google gives us reports on how the site is used, not your name or contact details. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Google Fonts. Our pages load their fonts from Google's servers, which see your IP address and browser when they send the files.

Because of these tools, Meta and Google may collect information about what you do on our site and on other sites over time. Some state privacy laws call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising." If you don't want yours used for ads, use the choices below.

Cookies

Snipcart uses cookies and your browser's storage to remember what's in your cart. Meta's pixel sets cookies to measure our ads, and Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. The newsletter form at the bottom of each page is run by Kit; its script loads only when you use the form, and sets a cookie at that point to remember your sign-up. The Apple Pay and Google Pay page loads Stripe, which uses cookies to prevent fraud.

Signing in to your account sets a cookie that keeps you signed in on that browser for 30 days, or until you sign out. Our account page doesn't load Meta's pixel.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, the cart can't remember what's in it, and you can't stay signed in.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no agreed standard for what a site should do with it, so ours doesn't change what it does when it gets one. The choices below still work.

Your Choices and Rights

  • See, fix, or delete your information: email us and we'll send you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. We keep what the law requires us to keep, like sales records for taxes. If you have an account, you can change your details or delete the account yourself on your account page.
  • Stop our emails: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or reminder email, or email us. Order and shipping emails still come while you have an order with us.
  • Limit ad tracking: change your Facebook ad preferences, install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on, block third-party cookies in your browser, or use a tracker blocker.

Depending on the state you live in, the law may give you more rights over your information, like getting a copy of it or having it deleted. We'll honor requests to see, fix, or delete your information wherever you live. Email us and we'll answer within 45 days. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act, and we won't treat you any differently for asking.

How Long We Keep It

We keep order records as long as we need them for taxes, warranty claims, and legal reasons. Our warranty runs for your career, so some order records stay a long time. Uploaded artwork and photos stay with the order or message they came with, under the same rules. An account stays until you delete it. Deleting it removes your details, your codes, and any saved card. We keep a scrambled one-way fingerprint of your email and street address. It can't be turned back into either one, and it's how we make sure the sign-up discount is only claimed once. When we no longer need something, we delete it or strip out what identifies you.

Security

Card payments are handled by Stripe and never touch our server, and that includes cards saved to an account. Account passwords are stored scrambled with a one-way hash, never as you typed them, and we can't see them. Use a password you don't use anywhere else, and keep your email secure, since a password reset goes to it. The shop's own tools sit behind a login. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but nothing online is 100% secure, and we can't promise it will never be breached. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children's Privacy

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. You have to be 18 or older to make an account. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date at the top.
